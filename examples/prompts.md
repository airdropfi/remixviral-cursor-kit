# Example prompts

Paste any of these into Cursor (Agent) after the RemixViral server shows a green dot in Settings, then MCP. Only the last kind spends credits, and it stops for your yes first.

1. `Show my RemixViral clip jobs and their status.`
2. `From my most recent RemixViral clip job, show the top 3 clips with start and end times and why each was picked.`
3. `Which RemixViral niches and art styles can I use for a faceless Short?`
4. `Show my 5 most recent RemixViral videos and their status.`
5. `How many RemixViral credits do I have left?`
6. `Make a 30-second faceless Short about why the moon looks bigger near the horizon. Check my balance, tell me the cost, and wait for my yes before creating it.`
